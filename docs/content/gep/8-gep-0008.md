# GEP-0008: accreditation — the gate, the verdict, the warrant

**Proposed — drafts the admission gate's normative core out of the 2026-07 research passes: the verdict value-space (closing GV2), the gate invariants, the criterion classes, the observation rules, and the warrant lifecycle. Acceptance does not close the signing-authority question. That one is constitutional, and it stays open on purpose.**

## Summary

An accreditation is a verdict in a two-axis space — grounding and warrant, never one bool — granted as a revocable warrant, not a terminal pass. The gate that grants it measures six criterion classes, each carrying a regime label (mechanical, triangulated-judgment, or human-ruling) and an exogeneity requirement on where its evidence comes from. Verdicts are evidence; authority is constitutional and never delegated to a score. Warrants ship with observation obligations attached; demotion is graduated; re-accreditation mints a successor, never mutates the original. One condition governs the whole design: **graded, revocable admission is sound exactly where the observation-and-demotion loop outruns harm.** Where it can't — one-way doors — the gate is conjunctive at full weight, and no track record lightens it.

## Motivation

The registry build is gated on this. The gap register put it plainly (G10): two-axis verdicts, over what value space? A content hash plus an authority plus a signature — of what, normalized how? Whether accreditation is a relation, a kind, or a side-table was unpicked. The doctrine had a wall — operators emit attestations, none mint warrants — and the wall was a sentence, not a mechanism.

So we went and measured what the naive gates actually do. Four research passes, every load-bearing claim independently verified against its source. The results are not close:

- **"Keep it if the score went up" is self-inflicted p-hacking.** Greedy acceptance against a reused noisy estimate commits false improvements 30–100% of the time in measurement. An e-process acceptor (commit only at E ≥ 1/α) drives that to roughly zero — and its guarantee is per-candidate, not per-run. Volume is a separate, open problem.
- **"The proxy tracks the true objective, so trust it" is false.** Reward hacking shows up while the proxy is *positively correlated* with truth. Past a computable point, more optimization pressure makes the real objective worse, and the published tolerance curves are explicitly fenced to the non-adversarial case. A gate is adversarial by construction.
- **"Signature valid, therefore trusted" has no exit.** The supply-chain attestation stack solved issuance and tamper-evidence. It did not solve revocation. A compromised-but-valid warrant passes forever unless something outside the signature chain is watching.
- **A judge calibrated at admission time is not calibrated later.** Judge confidence is measurably inflated; post-hoc correction fails under distribution shift; the fix is re-measurement against fresh ground truth, not threshold-tuning.

Each of these is the gate a reasonable engineer builds first. This GEP exists so we don't.

## Guide-level explanation

Walk one component through.

**Submission.** Structure runs first: signature against roots of trust, digest bound to the actual artifact, manifest well-formed. Mechanical, fail-closed, boring. A failure here is hard-stop and nothing downstream ever sees the candidate.

**Evidence.** The gate gathers what each criterion class needs — a behavioral fingerprint against the neighborhood of components providing the same thing (is this new, or a duplicate wearing a new name?), held-out outcome runs, proxy scores only where the true objective is too expensive to measure directly. None of it is authored by the proposer. The held-out sets, the reference distributions, the fingerprints: gate-owned, all of them.

**Verdict.** A cell in a lattice, not a number. Grounding on one axis (is the evidence chain intact?), warrant on the other (may it act?). Four cells, four autonomy ceilings.

**Probation.** Most first admissions land one cell short of full: warrant granted *with obligations attached* — standing telemetry designed to reveal what admission couldn't see. Going dark on an obligation is itself a demotion trigger. The channel self-sanctions.

**Demotion.** Graduated and severity-matched: flag, then a certified-but-suspended state, then revocation — never a skip straight to halt, escalation reserved for repetition. A defect surfaced in the field generalizes to the whole class that shares the type design, not just the instance that tripped.

**Re-accreditation.** A changed component is a new candidate. Mint a successor with a supersession edge and put it through the gate; never mutate the accredited artifact in place. Risk-reducing changes get a fast path. Meeting an obligation's *intent* a different way is allowed — if the gate approves the alternative, not if the author self-grants it.

## Reference-level explanation

### 1. The verdict value-space (resolves GV2)

Two graded axes: **grounding** (absent → partial → complete) and **warrant** (withheld → conditional → full). The enforced output is one of four cells:

| Verdict | Band | Grounding | Warrant |
|---|---|---|---|
| REJECT | hard-stop | absent, or reversibility floor tripped | withheld |
| REVISE-AND-SHIP | human-gate | a specific gap identified | withheld pending revision |
| SHIP-WITH-TIGHTENING | stage-for-review | present | conditional — obligations attached |
| SHIP | auto-apply | complete | full — and still revocable |

The gradient is real upstream — scoring, band selection, thresholds all work on continuous signals. The granted warrant is discrete. That is how graded and conjunctive coexist: grading lives on the axes, the ceiling is the enforcement.

### 2. Invariants

- **I1 — Verdicts are evidence, never authority.** Any check, human or machine, produces a checkable attestation. Authority to admit is constitutional and sits with the minting human. A check that grants its own warrant is malformed.
- **I2 — The proposer never authors the observation channel.** References, held-out sets, fingerprints, e-process wealth: gate-owned. Evidence must come from at least one rung of exogeneity away from the proposer; one-way doors require substrate exogeneity — mechanical execution or human ruling, the only rungs whose failures are uncorrelated with the proposer's. The rungs: 0 self, 1 another agent, 2 another model family, 3 substrate. Correlation between rungs is measured, not assumed.
- **I3 — Accuracy is empirical; authority is constitutional.** Human and model verdicts are both scored against ground truth, per decision class, on a running ledger. Delegate reversible gates where the data says delegation is safe. Never delegate a one-way door, at any accuracy. And the ratifier's engagement is itself observable — a rubber-stamp voids the warrant it stamps.

### 3. Criterion classes

| # | Class | Measures | Regime | Exogeneity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | structural / provenance | signature, digest binding, manifest shape | mechanical | substrate |
| C2 | behavioral novelty | fingerprint equivalence vs the provides-neighborhood | mechanical evidence → human ruling | substrate / human |
| C3 | proxy-scored quality | calibrated proxy where truth is expensive | triangulated-judgment | family, minimum |
| C4 | held-out outcomes | multi-sample runs, mechanically scored | mechanical | substrate |
| C5 | authority / one-way-door | is this decision irreversible or accountability-bearing? | human-ruling | human |
| C6 | verifier self-state | a judgment component's own configuration probe | triangulated-judgment | agent, at best |

C2's flag rule: embedding similarity flags, never verdicts — the measured false-positive rate on similarity-as-verdict was total. C6 is early-warning only, never load-bearing alone; the observable is also a steering target, which makes it Goodhartable by construction.

### 4. Floors

- **Reversibility floor.** If a false pass is unrecoverable — downstream actors move before re-review, or the human-accountability surface is touched — the gate is conjunctive pass/fail at full weight, every time. SHIP-WITH-TIGHTENING is incoherent at a one-way door: the tightening is post-hoc observation, and observation cannot recover a pass that already escaped.
- **Novelty floor.** New components, out-of-family work, high-variance classes: full conjunctive weight regardless of record.
- **Fail-safe.** If band selection cannot be made deterministic for a case, it reverts to conjunctive. The selector must never become an ungated gate.

### 5. Calibration obligations (C3, and anywhere a proxy scores)

A proxy row must carry: a characterized error distribution against gold, not a point accuracy — below the labeled-data floor the proxy is near-chance and must be treated as noise; a cap on how hard the proxy was optimized against, because tightening the proxy cannot close the gap that optimizing it opens; a standing drift monitor — two-sample test of live score distributions against the accreditation-time reference, with malignancy checked separately from detection; anytime-valid acceptance where candidates arrive as a stream; and a recalibration trigger driven by observed miscoverage per decision class, not a calendar. Under large shift, recalibration means fresh ground-truth labels. Post-hoc correction is known to fail there.

### 6. Observation rules

- **OR1** — the proposer never authors, hosts, or improves the channel (I2 restated as an operational rule).
- **OR2** — gaming detection compares against a trusted exogenous baseline, never the candidate's self-score. And the baseline detectors are themselves measurably weak; the observer is fallible and the design assumes so.
- **OR3** — measure informativeness, not just pass-rate. A coverage-style gate can keep its number by admitting everything.
- **OR4** — pair trend monitoring with anomaly detection. Misalignment can arrive as a discontinuous jump; slow-drift watching misses it.
- **OR5** — the channel self-sanctions. Telemetry going dark is a demotion trigger in its own right, before any bad outcome.
- **OR6** — the ledger dedups at entry, marks staleness, and supersedes by appending. Nothing is rewritten.

### 7. The record

An accreditation is an **append-only ledger entry**, keyed by the component's `type_url` and content digest. Fields: subject (identity + digest), per-criterion evidence references, the verdict cell, the attached obligations, the minting authority, and a signature over the normalized record. It is not a manifest field, and the reason is structural: a warrant living inside the artifact could only be revoked by mutating the artifact, and accredited artifacts do not mutate — supersession is already the ruled evolution path. Demotion is a later entry; the component's current standing is the fold of its ledger. This answers the register's open trichotomy — relation, kind, or side-table — with: side-table, authoritative; plus an optional relations edge for discoverability that carries no authority whatsoever.

### 8. The anchor rule

Any part of the gate that adapts — thresholds, judges, evolving criteria — must keep an anchor the proposer loop cannot train on. Every working co-evolution design in the literature we verified keeps one; the designs differ only in where they put it. Held-out ground truth is spent, not reused. And one pre-registered canary: an evolving admission criterion whose pass-rate approaches 1.0 has collapsed into vacuity, not succeeded. Treat it as a defect surfaced in the field.

## Drawbacks

The observation channel is standing, funded infrastructure: references, held-out sets, fingerprints, telemetry, a ledger — maintained for as long as the catalog lives. That cost is the price of revocability, and it is real.

The guarantees we can actually buy are per-candidate. A proposer submitting enough non-improving candidates accrues false commits at rate ≤ α each; no whole-loop safeguard exists yet. Registered as a residual, not solved.

Two pieces of vocabulary ride in as organization rather than evidence: the four bands come from an internal cluster that was never independently verified, and the graduated-sanctions ladder is lifted from a commons-governance configuration whose preconditions — repeat play, persistent reputation, cheap mutual observation — are untested for agent ecosystems. Both are marked as such in the evidence dossier.

The judgment rows need calibration machinery that does not exist yet. Until it does, C3 and C6 run at human-gate, which means the near-term gate is more conservative and more human-expensive than the design's steady state.

## Rationale and alternatives

- **One bool.** We already shipped this mistake once — a single asserted flag conflating "grounded" with "authorized" is the incident that produced the two-axis rule. Rejected permanently.
- **Conjunctive everywhere.** Honest, and it forfeits throughput exactly where the evidence says grading is sound. Worse, it routes all volume to the human, and an overloaded ratifier degrades into the rubber-stamp that I3 exists to catch. Refusing to grade doesn't remove the risk; it relocates it to the least observable place.
- **A continuous autonomy dial.** The gradient is real and it survives — inside scoring and band selection. But enforcement needs a discrete ceiling; a warrant you can hold 0.7 of is a warrant nobody can check.
- **Trust-forever on signature.** The measured negative case. Issuance without revocation is how a compromised warrant outlives its compromise.
- **An LLM judge as the authority.** A gate you can persuade is not a gate. Judges produce evidence under I1; the warrant is minted above them.

## Prior art

FDA device regulation (conditions of approval attached at or after admission; postmarket surveillance plans judged by whether they can reveal the unforeseen; a graduated enforcement ladder up to withdrawal, with the top rung bounded, not automatic). FAA airworthiness (type certification revocable post-hoc by directive; triggers that generalize to every product of the type design; a certified-but-unairworthy suspension state; and on conflict, the directive supersedes the manufacturer's own document it incorporated — the gate outranks the artifact it was built from). Ostrom's graduated sanctions (first sanctions surprisingly low; escalation reserved for repetition; the deterrent is reputation plus repeat play, not severity). SLSA / in-toto / Sigstore as the negative case on revocation. Gao et al. on reward-model overoptimization and Pan et al. on reward misspecification (the proxy evidence). PACE and the safe-anytime-valid-inference line (e-process acceptance). Gibbs & Candès (adaptive conformal — the miscoverage-triggered cadence). Tian et al. on judge calibration; Ovadia et al. on calibration under shift. "Who Grades the Grader?" for the anchor topology and the vacuity canary.

*The full evidence dossier — extractions, independent verification passes, audits — lives in the research tree (`gate-design-rubric`, `self-evolving-agents` and its evaluator-coevolution addendum, `ratchet-curation`), with every claim in this GEP traceable to a verified source claim.*

## Unresolved questions

- **Who mints.** Today: the human, constitutionally, and the wall stands — operators emit attestations, none mint warrants. Whether minting authority ever gets shared machinery (a quorum, a second keyholder) is open past acceptance and is the blocking question for final acceptance of the record's signing semantics.
- **The run-level guarantee.** Per-candidate error control under proposer volume; online false-discovery control and α-investing are the fetch direction.
- **The adversarial cadence.** How fast a strategic proposer degrades a judge's calibration is unmeasured anywhere. The pre-registered verifier-error-correlation experiment is the first instrument aimed at it.
- **Crystallization.** What promotes a judgment-rung check into a cached mechanical rule — observation count, agreement rate, outcome parity, sign-off — is unspecified.
- **Timelines at agent speed.** The regulatory precedent assumes a slow world (plans in 30 days, studies in 36 months). What probation obligations compress to when the proposer iterates in minutes is undesigned.
- **Reputation across supersession.** Graduated sanctions deter only if standing persists; whether a lineage of superseding components carries reputation coherently is open.

## Future possibilities

The calibration lab: per-decision-class scoring of human and model verdicts on the ledger, which is what eventually lets reversible gates delegate on evidence instead of posture. Verifier-state recusal probes as an early-warning input to C6, if the adversarial-robustness gap ever closes. A verification-summary import from the supply-chain world once the revocation re-fetch lands. And the composed gate as a catalog citizen: the gate's own criterion classes are components, which means one day the gate is accredited by the process it runs — with its anchor, per the anchor rule, held out of reach.
