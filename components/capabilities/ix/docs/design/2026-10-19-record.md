# ix design record, 2026-10-19: the statistics of a verdict, and the contracts around it

The record of ix's design judgments, kept in passes. The first two (2026-10-19) brought
the verdict's statistics (§1-6) and the agent path (§7-15) to an externally usable
standard. The later ones (2026-10-22) corrected the analysis (§16), split the runtime ports
(§17), tied a verdict to what it judged (§18-19), settled reading, roots and placeholders
(§20), put requirements in place of effects (§21), added environments (§22), checked the
layers (§23), added the evaluator sensor (§24), asked runtimes what their sessions do
(§25), and closed what the final review found (§26). Where a later pass makes an earlier
row untrue, the earlier row is amended in place and the later one says why.

It follows the record form of slick's `crafting-aces` skill: one row per judgment, each
with the test it answers to, its verdict, the weakness or measure it leaves, and its
evidence, tagged *observed* (read or run at this snapshot), *reported* (a document, not
re-observed), *inferred* or *hypothesis*. Code paths are relative to
`components/capabilities/ix/`.

The first pass began from a comparison of ix against comparable tools (a research note of
2026-10-19, its §6.2, *reported*) and three simulations that called ix's own analysis
functions unchanged. They were run again before any change here, with the same results
(*observed*, table in §2).

Findings cited by name (`ix.md`, `full/ix-domain.md`, `full/ix-outer.md`,
`full/ix-tests.md`, "the inventory") are review and research documents of 2026-10-19 and
2026-10-22 kept outside the repository; each row states what was found and what was
decided, so it stands without them. Ids such as W8.6 name the item of the owner's plan of
work a row answers. The owner's rulings the rows follow are these:

- **Q1**, owner ruling (yzavyas, 2026-10-22): spend is removed and not replaced. Tokens stay
  as usage facts; a cost a provider reports is kept only as a fact of the session, never
  limited and never estimated.
- **Q3**, owner ruling (yzavyas, 2026-10-22): new in this release are matrix's pydantic-ai
  agent runtime and pydantic-evals' evaluators as ix sensors.
- **Q4**, owner ruling (yzavyas, 2026-10-22): the OpenTelemetry SDK and OTLP/http are default
  dependencies of the CLIs and the libraries stay API-only; pydantic-evals' span capture
  works with the plain SDK provider, so Logfire is not needed.
- **Q6**, owner ruling (yzavyas, 2026-10-22): effects are removed from ix and matrix; each
  adapter's checked requirements take their place, and a stand-in is what a runtime
  declares.
- **Q7**, owner ruling (yzavyas, 2026-10-22), reversing a proposal to remove it:
  `environment.compose` is implemented, for experiments of several services.
- **Q11**, owner ruling (yzavyas, 2026-10-22): factors drive agent settings: a level keeps its
  type, a subject's placeholders are validated, and each cell's options are reviewed.
- **Q13**, owner ruling (yzavyas, 2026-10-22): no package has optional extras; every runtime
  and the OpenTelemetry SDK are default dependencies, their heavy imports lazy.

## 1. The claim

Written R(X, Y, W, M): system X keeps property Y against perturbation class W by measure M.

| | |
|---|---|
| X | ix's analysis: `src/ix/domain/analysis.py` over the methods in `src/ix/domain/intervals.py` |
| Y | for the person who gates a change on ix's exit code: a decision ix calls `holds` is wrong no more often than the one-sided 2.5% it states, except in two cases held to bounds of their own: a pass rate on its threshold (Wilson), at most 3.9% over 5 to 200 trials (§3), and a pooled count on its threshold with failures clustered in trials, at most 3.9% in the cases simulated (§16); a printed 95% interval covers the truth about 95% of the time, and a verdict is never certain because of how a number was stored |
| W | per-trial noise of normal and moderately skewed shape; the trial counts the genres accept (5 to 20 blocks, 3 for `characterize`); failures that cluster within a trial; families of decisions across variants, cells and guardrails |
| M | rates measured by simulation with fixed seeds through `analyze()` itself (`tests/test_calibration.py`: a fast set in the normal suite, the full set with `IX_SLOW=1 pytest -m slow`), each held to three Monte Carlo standard errors of its stated value; the Wilson bound is computed exactly, and the count-on-threshold rows of §16 are simulated on the interval's level (`clustered_rate`), not through `analyze()`, so they would not catch a fault in how the analysis picks a count's level |

The claim is not that the analysis is ACES; it is a claim of calibration, and the rows
below are its warrant.

## 5. Verdicts in existing tests that changed, and why

- `test_a_difference_inside_the_noise_floor_decides_nothing` became
  `..._inside_the_noise_of_the_trials_...`: the noise now widens the interval through the
  spread of the per-block differences; nothing is added to it.
- `test_decisions_are_corrected_together`: a `holds` at 0.03 beside another decision held
  no longer; it holds now (intersection-union). Two `fails` at 0.03 and 0.04 still do not.
- The bootstrap-floor tests became `test_a_decision_far_beyond_the_boundary_reports_its_tests_level`
  (a p-value below 1e-10 where the bound was 2/4999).
- `test_a_changed_experiment_runs_and_its_deviations_are_in_the_report` became
  `..._runs_ungated_until_its_registration_is_amended` (§4).
- Counts that followed the old default repeats (5 for `compare`, 3 for `evaluate`) follow
  the new ones; the fake experiments in `tests/ix_fakes.py` set the minimum, 5.
- No verdict on readings that a test fixes by hand flipped between `holds` and `fails`.

# Second pass, 2026-10-19: matrix's loader, agent evaluation, telemetry

The second of the two passes: ix reads what an author writes through matrix 0.6.0's loader,
runs agent evaluations again on matrix's agent runtimes, and traces its own work. Rows take
the same form as above. "The inventory" is a research note of 2026-10-19 that listed what
an agent evaluation needs (*reported*); item ids (A1, D3, ...) are its. The earlier ix is `git show ee0ffdd^:components/capabilities/ix/`.
