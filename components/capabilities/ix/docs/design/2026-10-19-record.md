# ix design record, 2026-10-19: the statistics of a verdict, and the contracts around it

The record of the first of two passes that bring ix to an externally usable standard. It
follows the record form of slick's `crafting-aces` skill: one row per judgment, each with
the test it answers to, its verdict, the weakness or measure it leaves, and its evidence,
tagged *observed* (read or run at this snapshot), *reported* (a document, not re-observed),
*inferred* or *hypothesis*. Code paths are relative to `components/capabilities/ix/`.

The evidence that started it is the comparison of ix against the field
(`scratch/research-matrix-ix-landscape/2026-10-19/ix-comparison.md` §6.2, *reported*) and
three simulations that called ix's own analysis functions unchanged. They were run again
before any change here, with the same results (*observed*, table in §2).

## 1. The claim

Written R(X, Y, W, M): system X keeps property Y against perturbation class W by measure M.

| | |
|---|---|
| X | ix's analysis: `src/ix/domain/analysis.py` over the methods in `src/ix/domain/intervals.py` |
| Y | for the person who gates a change on ix's exit code: a decision ix calls `holds` is wrong no more often than the one-sided 2.5% it states, a printed 95% interval covers the truth about 95% of the time, and a verdict is never certain because of how a number was stored |
| W | per-trial noise of normal and moderately skewed shape; the trial counts the genres accept (5 to 20 blocks, 3 for `characterize`); failures that cluster within a trial; families of decisions across variants, cells and guardrails |
| M | rates measured by simulation with fixed seeds through `analyze()` itself (`tests/test_calibration.py`: a fast set in the normal suite, the full set with `IX_SLOW=1 pytest -m slow`), each held to three Monte Carlo standard errors of its stated value |

The claim is not that the analysis is ACES; it is a claim of calibration, and the rows
below are its warrant.

### Kept, dropped, and why

| what | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|
| `decided_at` | kept | Holm's procedure needs each decision's level; it is now the test's p-value where the interval inverts a test (t, Fieller, Wilson, the clustered rate, order statistics), searched only for Newcombe's difference |
| `allowed` | kept | the full 0.05 for `holds`, Holm's step for `fails` |
| `at_floor`, the per-method floor, the 125-decision bound | dropped | they were the percentile bootstrap's resolution (2 / 4999); no method left has one. A searched level stops at 1e-6, which Holm reaches only past 50,000 decisions |

## 5. Verdicts in existing tests that changed, and why

- `test_a_difference_inside_the_noise_floor_decides_nothing` became
  `..._inside_the_noise_of_the_trials_...`: the noise now widens the interval through the
  spread of the per-block differences; nothing is added to it.
- `test_decisions_are_corrected_together`: a `holds` at 0.03 beside another decision held
  no longer; it holds now (intersection-union). Two `fails` at 0.03 and 0.04 still do not.
- The bootstrap-floor tests became `test_a_decision_far_beyond_the_boundary_reports_its_tests_level`
  (a p-value below 1e-10 where the bound was 2/4999).
- `test_a_changed_experiment_runs_and_its_deviations_are_in_the_report` became
  `..._runs_ungated_until_its_registration_is_amended` (§4).
- Counts that followed the old default repeats (5 for `compare`, 3 for `evaluate`) follow
  the new ones; the fake experiments in `tests/ix_fakes.py` set the minimum, 5.
- No verdict on readings that a test fixes by hand flipped between `holds` and `fails`.
