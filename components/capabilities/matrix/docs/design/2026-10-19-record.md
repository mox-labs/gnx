# matrix 0.6.0 and 0.7.0: design record, 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-22

The judgments made building matrix 0.6.0 and 0.7.0, recorded by the crafting-aces procedure (its
`SKILL.md`, and `references/ports-and-adapters.md`, `interface.md`, `composition-root.md`).
Rows take the skill's form: claim, test, verdict, named weakness or measure, evidence.
Evidence tags: *observed* (read or run on this tree today), *reported* (a dated document),
*inferred*, *hypothesis*. "The audit" is the audit of matrix's and ix's CLI and API of
2026-10-19, a working note kept outside the repository (reported). "The review" is the
review of matrix of 2026-10-22, with its full reads of the sources and of the tests, also
kept outside the repository.

Provenance carried from the skill: the ports-and-adapters layout rests on one author and one
adopting study; the composition root driven by configuration is the ACES framework's
practice, not the canon's; stasis, drag and opacity are that framework's model. Nothing here
is called ACES compliant; each claim is stated in four parts and held by its tests.
